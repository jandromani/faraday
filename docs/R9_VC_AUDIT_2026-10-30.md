## Blockers before 75 becomes defensible

1. Independent deployment of real Keycloak/OIDC and Vault, production-grade
   rotated workload identity, no-bypass network boundary and security review.
2. Replace Docker exec-based SQL harness with least-privilege TLS PostgreSQL
   driver, automatic leases/election, independently crashed backend, true
   link partitions, split-brain rejection and measured RPO/RTO across failures.
3. Confirm effects on an **authorized actual downstream service**, with
   downstream idempotency and reconciliation of unknown outcomes; no real
   money movement should be used to test.
4. Get five unrelated clean installs, one consensual non-production pilot and
   one independent pen-test, without fabricating usage or certifications.
   Track [R10 #34](https://github.com/jandromani/faraday/issues/34) and
   [distributed HA #35](https://github.com/jandromani/faraday/issues/35).
5. Add individual critical CI job names directly to master ruleset; transitive
   required security checks are in place but administrator-owned rules should
   eventually cover R7, R6 and R1 explicitly.
